# Store artwork

## Current icon links

Updated on 2026-09-29. All three stores use an icon-only link with a 50-by-50
CSS pixel image box, original artwork proportions, and 20 pixels of clear
space on every side. No additional backing panel, recoloring, or filters are
applied. Each link opens the corresponding game's listing in a new tab and
has an accessible label naming the game and store. Trademark credits appear
in the footer.

- `app-store-icon.png`: unchanged official Apple artwork downloaded from
  https://developer.apple.com/assets/elements/icons/app-store/app-store-128x128_2x.png
  - App Store resources: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/
- `google-play-icon.svg`: the four symbol paths extracted from the official
  Google Play badge below. Original path geometry and colors are retained;
  lettering and badge background are omitted, with a square view box around
  the triangle.
- `steam.svg`: the first three symbol paths extracted from Valve's official
  SVG, retaining their original geometry and color. Wordmark paths are
  omitted, with a square view box around the symbol.
  - Source: https://store.fastly.steamstatic.com/public/shared/images/header/logo_steam.svg
  - Guidelines: https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/branding
  - Guidelines PDF: https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/community_assets/images/steamworks_docs/english/steam_brandGuidelines.pdf

Shared interaction follows https://solilius.github.io/portfolio/: links grow
to 110% on hover or keyboard focus, and shrink to 90% while pressed, using a
0.2-second ease-in-out transition. Reduced-motion preferences disable both
the transition and scaling.

## Original mobile badges retained

Downloaded on 2026-09-28 and stored unchanged from their official sources.
These original full badges are retained as source artwork and are not used
by the current icon-only links.

- `app-store.svg`: https://developer.apple.com/assets/elements/badges/download-on-the-app-store.svg
  - Guidelines: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/marketing/guidelines/
- `google-play.svg`: `Google Play Badge guidelines/Get it on Google Play Badges/Digital/svg/GetItOnGooglePlay_Badge_Web_color_English.svg`
  in the official download pack from
  https://partnermarketinghub.withgoogle.com/brands/google-play/google-play/lockups-icons-badges/

Artwork remains owned by Apple, Google, and Valve respectively and is subject
to their usage terms. Asset provenance does not establish licensing eligibility
