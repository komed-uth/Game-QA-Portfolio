Downloaded on 2026-09-28. Files are stored unchanged from their official sources.

  - Guidelines: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/marketing/guidelines/

  - Guidelines and asset download: https://partnermarketinghub.withgoogle.com/brands/google-play/google-play/lockups-icons-badges/

Display rules used in the showcase:

- Preferred black App Store badge first.
- Both badges 44 CSS pixels high, with their original aspect ratios.
- At least one quarter of badge height as clear space (12 pixels).
- Dark page background with sufficient contrast; no separate backing panel,
  cropping, recoloring, effects, or hover animation.
- Each badge links directly to the corresponding Regulus the Advent listing.
- Trademark credit lines in the site footer.
