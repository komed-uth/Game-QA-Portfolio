# Game QA Portfolio

Portfolio language for presenting game projects and the work associated with them.

## Language

**Project showcase**:
A portfolio section devoted to one game, presenting its overview, the contributor's role and responsibilities, platforms and tools, and project media.

**Skills page**:
A portfolio page presenting the contributor's QA capabilities, working tools, and approach to testing, with supporting project evidence where available.

**QA approach**:
The contributor's reasoning and workflow for testing games, presented as part of the Skills page.

**Gameplay showcase**:
Project media selected to illustrate gameplay situations and interfaces, with captions describing visible content.
